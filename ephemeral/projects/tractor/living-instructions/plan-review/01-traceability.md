# Pass 1: traceability

Question: does every promise in `promises.md` reach at least one chapter
or sprint item in `chapters.md` and the chapter ledgers, and does every
chapter and sprint item serve at least one promise? List each promise
with the items that serve it and each item with the promises it serves.
An item serving no promise is speculative scope; a promise no item
serves is unbuilt. Either is a fail. Name the owning node: `decompose`
for a missing route, `brief` for a promise that should not exist.
