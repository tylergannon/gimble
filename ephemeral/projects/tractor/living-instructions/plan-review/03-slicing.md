# Pass 3: slicing

Question: is every chapter, and every sprint in chapter 4's ledger and
chapters 5 and 6's backlog sketches, a vertical slice: when it is done,
something is exercisable end to end and demonstrates at least one
promise or de-risks a seam a promise crosses? Reject horizontal
stack-order plans (build all the data layer, then all the services,
then all the UI). For each item say what is exercisable at its end. A
slice with nothing exercisable is a fail; owning node `decompose`.
