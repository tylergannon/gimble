# The pyramid index

Long documents need a way to be skimmed without being read. Put a
pyramid index near the top: reversible summaries at three zoom levels,
so a reader can stop at the level that answers their question and a
planner can scan every chapter by reading only the indexes.

- **L0.** One sentence naming the vector. If a reader reads nothing
  else, this is what they know.
- **L1.** Three to five bullets: the major directions or claims. Each
  should be recoverable from the body and the body from it.
- **L2.** Pointers to the sections that expand each L1 bullet, by
  heading.

Reversible means the index is a compression of the body, not a second
body. Do not put anything in the index that the body does not say, and
do not repeat the body's detail in the index.

Use it on chapter docs always, on briefs and research indexes when
they pass a screen, and never on sprint docs, which are read whole.
